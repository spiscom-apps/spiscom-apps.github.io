Orbiroad terms of use

Version 1 · effective 26 September 2026

You accept these terms on the app's start screen. Orbiroad records the road and assists the driver, but it does not replace the driver's attention or judgement.

1. Information only

  • Speed limits, signs and speed cameras shown by the app are for information and assistance only. They come from image recognition and the open OpenStreetMap map, so they may be incomplete, outdated or wrong.
  • Road signs and signals, instructions from people directing traffic, and traffic law always take precedence.

2. Driving safety

  • Do not operate the phone while driving. Set the app up before you set off.
  • Mount the phone securely so that it does not block your view or get in the way of airbags.

3. Recordings and the law

  • Recording video and audio may be subject to data protection law. Use recordings lawfully. See the privacy policy for details.
  • Warning about speed cameras is prohibited in some countries. Automatic mode turns warnings off in countries where the app knows of such a ban, but you remain responsible for following the rules of the country you are driving in.

4. Technical limits

Recording depends on your phone: its camera, storage, battery and temperature, and the manufacturer's power-saving settings. The app may pause recording when the battery is low or the phone overheats. We do not guarantee that every incident will be recorded. Check regularly that recording works.

5. Orbiroad Pro

  • Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase with no subscription. It unlocks longer loops, 1440p and 4K resolution, and high image quality. All other features are free.
  • Pro is free as a trial for 7 days from the first installation.
  • Payments, refunds and restoring the purchase on a new phone are handled by Google Play on the same Google account, under Google Play's terms.

6. Liability

The app is provided as is. To the extent permitted by law, Spiscom is not liable for damage resulting from inaccurate speed limit, sign or speed camera information, or from a missing recording. This does not affect consumer rights that cannot be limited by contract.

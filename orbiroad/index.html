Turn your old phone into a dashcam

The phone sitting in your drawer can record every drive. Orbiroad can run completely offline: no ads, no account and no data sent anywhere. Your recordings stay on your phone.

A second life for an old phone

Runs on Android 7.0 and newer, including phones that are years old, with no SIM card and no internet. Use a phone you already own instead of buying a dashcam.

Fully offline

Recording, incidents, parking mode and sign recognition work without internet. Download the speed limit and speed camera map once over Wi-Fi and use it offline.

No ads, no tracking

No ads, no analytics, no account and no servers of ours. Recordings never leave your phone. The only connection is the optional OpenStreetMap download, which you can turn off.

How it works with an old phone

  1. Install Orbiroad, mount the phone on the windscreen and connect it to a car charger.
  2. In “Start recording”, choose “When power is connected”. Recording starts by itself when the socket gets power and stops when power is disconnected.
  3. Recordings are saved in a loop and the oldest are deleted automatically. After an impact, the clip is protected from overwriting.

The app watches the battery and temperature: when the phone overheats it lowers quality, and at critical temperature it pauses and then resumes recording on its own.

Features

Loop recording

720p – 4K, H.265 / H.264, 1–5 min files with no gaps, storage and age limits, recording in the background with the screen off.

Incidents

Impact detection, on-screen button, widget, volume keys. Footage before and after the incident is protected from overwriting.

Parking mode

Low-power accelerometer watch or motion detection in the frame. An impact saves a clip with pre-event buffer.

Signs and map

Speed limit signs read by the camera, limits and speed cameras from OpenStreetMap, offline map.

Driving screen

Speed, G-meter, 0–100 timing, break reminder, km/h or mph by country.

Footage as evidence

GPS in every file, overlay with date and speed, SRT/GPX export, SHA-256 verification.

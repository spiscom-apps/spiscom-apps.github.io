Frequently asked questions

Recording stops after a while

Some manufacturers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei) aggressively put apps to sleep. In Android settings, turn off battery optimisation for Orbiroad (Apps → Orbiroad → Battery → Unrestricted). On Xiaomi phones, also enable autostart.

Recording does not start on its own when power is connected

Android does not allow starting the camera from the background. Orbiroad works around this with the “Display over other apps” permission, which you need to grant in settings.

Where are my recordings?

In the app's recordings browser. The files are in your chosen storage location: phone storage, SD card or a selected folder. “Save to gallery” puts a copy in Movies/Orbiroad.

Why is the speed limit wrong or missing?

The sign may have been hidden, dirty or poorly lit, and OpenStreetMap data can be incomplete. The app is for information only, so the road sign always takes precedence. You can add missing limits to OpenStreetMap.

I don't see speed camera warnings

In automatic mode, warnings are turned off in countries where they are prohibited or restricted: Germany, France, Switzerland, Cyprus and North Macedonia.

Do I need a SIM card or internet?

No. Recording, incidents, parking mode and sign recognition work without a SIM card or network. You need internet only once, to download the speed limit and speed camera map over Wi-Fi (Settings → “Download an area for offline use”). You can turn off fetching missing areas while driving. Buying Pro requires a connection to Google Play.

How do I prepare an old phone?

Reset it to factory settings, install only Orbiroad and keep it connected to a car charger. In summer, don't leave the phone behind the windscreen in the sun while parked. Old batteries tolerate heat poorly, and if the battery is swollen, don't use the phone. A charger of at least 2 A and a U3 / V30 memory card, if the phone has a slot, work best.

I bought Pro on another phone

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account and open the app. The purchase is restored automatically.

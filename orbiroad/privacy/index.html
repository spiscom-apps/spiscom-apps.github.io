Orbiroad privacy policy

Version 1 · effective 26 September 2026 · applies to the Orbiroad Android app (com.spiscom.dashcam)

2. Summary

  • Orbiroad has no user accounts, no developer servers, no ads and no analytics. Spiscom does not receive your recordings, location or any other app data.
  • Recordings and sensor data are stored only on your phone, on an SD card or in a folder you choose.
  • The only data sent over the internet are OpenStreetMap map queries for the area around you (without any user identifier) and Google Play handling your Orbiroad Pro purchase.
  • The app does not recognise faces or licence plates.

4. When data leaves your phone

a) OpenStreetMap data (Overpass API)

If you turn on map speed limits or speed camera warnings, or download an offline map, the app sends a public Overpass API server a query for roads within a rectangle of about 5 × 3.5 km. The server receives the rectangle's coordinates, your IP address and the app name with its version (e.g. Orbiroad/0.1.0). From this, your approximate area can be inferred. No user or device identifier is sent.

Servers, in order, with the later ones used only when the earlier ones do not respond: overpass-api.de (Germany), maps.mail.ru (Russia), overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee. They are run by independent operators under their own terms. If a query goes to a server outside the EU, your IP address and approximate area are transferred to that country.

To limit this, turn off “Fetch missing areas over the internet” and use a map downloaded in advance, or turn off map limits and speed cameras.

b) Google Play – Orbiroad Pro purchase

Payment is handled by Google through Google Play. The app only receives the purchase confirmation (product ID and purchase token) and verifies it on the phone. We never see your payment details. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

c) Live view (monitoring camera)

This feature is off by default. When you turn it on, the phone streams image and audio (RTSP and a browser page) to devices that provide the password you set. Connections are accepted only from the local network and VPN networks (e.g. Tailscale), never through Spiscom servers. Tailscale is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

d) Sharing recordings

A recording, SRT subtitles or a GPX route goes wherever you send it with Android's share function.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Loop recordings are overwritten automatically according to your storage, age and length limits.
  • Incident recordings are kept until you delete them or they exceed the separate incident limits.
  • You can delete everything in the recordings browser. “Delete the downloaded map” removes the offline map.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its settings and internal data. Recordings saved in a public folder, on an SD card, in a chosen folder or copied to the gallery may remain and must be deleted manually.

7. Your recordings and other people's rights

Recordings may contain other people's faces, licence plates and passengers' voices, which are personal data. You are responsible for how you use them.

  • Before publishing a recording, blur the faces and licence plates of bystanders.
  • Tell passengers that audio is being recorded, or mute it.
  • Some countries restrict dashcam use or audio recording (e.g. Austria, Luxembourg), and warning about speed cameras may be prohibited. Check the rules of the country you are driving in.

8. Legal basis and your rights

Spiscom does not collect data from the app, so there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. You control the data on your phone. Map queries are sent to provide a feature you have chosen (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to object, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country. If you e-mail us, we keep your address and message only to reply.

9. Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 16.

10. Security

Live view requires a password and accepts connections only from private addresses and VPN networks. A recording's SHA-256 checksum confirms that the file has not been changed since it was saved. Keep your phone's screen lock on, because recordings are accessible to anyone with access to the device.

11. Changes

We will announce significant changes on this page and in the app. Each version has a number and an effective date.
